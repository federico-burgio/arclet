Configuration storage

Chrome Sync must be enabled in Chrome on each device, using the same Google account and Arclet extension. Arclet cannot verify Chrome’s account or Sync settings. Offline changes stay on this device until Chrome can deliver them.

Arclet uses an 80 KB working limit. At 80% usage, a storage ring appears beside Settings in the sidebar. To save a configuration larger than the limit, explicitly disable Sync first. Disabling it keeps your local configuration and does not turn it off on other devices.

Help & about

A little guidance for a calmer browser.

Find anything

⌘ K while the sidebar has focus.

Open the sidebar with ⌘ ⇧ E.

Everything in its place

Drag a tab to reorder it or move it to a space. Right-click a space or New tab to create a pinned folder or restore a closed tab. Right-click saved pages to customize icons, organize pins, or add favorites. Drag pins and folder headings to reorder them. Drop pins onto a folder’s center to file them, or near its edges to place them beside it. Use Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ to reorder with the keyboard. Drag favorites to rearrange them, or use Alt+← / Alt+→. Open-tab menus can close all tabs or all tabs below.

Saved tabs stay yours

A return arrow means browsing away from the saved URL. Home and closed items have no status badge; hover for their state and current address. Click an open item to resume its current page. Closing a pinned tab keeps the pin; reopen it to start at home. Right-click for Return to saved URL, Unpin page, or Remove favorite.

Just you and your tabs

Saved pages and spaces stay on this device unless you enable optional Chrome Sync. Sync uses your Chrome account; Arclet has no account system, analytics, or backend.

For a left sidebar, choose Left in Chrome’s side panel appearance settings. Chrome’s tab strip stays visible. Tabs return to their spaces when Chrome restores your previous session. Enable “Continue where you left off” in Chrome’s startup settings.

Enable Chrome Sync?

The chosen configuration can replace the copy on other syncing devices. A recovery copy of the configuration being replaced is kept on this device.

Enabling Sync stores your saved URLs, titles, folders, and preferences in Chrome Sync, which can send them to Google and your other signed-in Chrome devices. No live tabs, browsing history, cookies, or passwords are included.

Disable Sync on this device?

Your configuration stays on this device. Future changes will be local, including configurations over 80 KB. Other devices and the copy already in Chrome are unchanged.

Import into Arclet

Spaces and folders with the same name are merged. Existing pages, their order, and their appearance are kept; new items are appended. No browser tabs are opened or closed.
